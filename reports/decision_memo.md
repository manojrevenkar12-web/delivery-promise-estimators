# Decision memo: estimators for route planning and picking

_Generated by `python -m dpe.eval.cli evaluate` from the development hubs. Do not edit by hand._

## In short

On the same simulated orders, streets and absences, the platform's estimators cut late deliveries from 198 to 33 a day per hub (14.5% to 2.5% of accepted orders). They do it partly by turning more orders away when the hub is full (1.6% to 5.3%): the planner no longer accepts orders it cannot deliver on time. Net of driver hours, the hub saves €18,928 a month against today (95% interval of the change −€25,078 to −€12,611), at €6 per late delivery and €9.5 per order turned away.

On the held-out hubs the gate passed.
